A warm farewell to visiting PhD student Jiazheng Zhang, who is leaving us after his research visit to Bristol, hosted by Wei Wei and John Cartlidge. Since joining us in October 2025, Jiazheng has been exploring blockchain-enabled incentive mechanisms for electricity data assets, resulting in the research preprint *Incentive Alignment for Electricity Data Assets in China: A Blockchain-Enabled Contribution-Credit Framework*, co-authored by Jiazheng Zhang, Shouwei Li, Yutian Deng, Wei Wei, and John Cartlidge ([available: arXiv:2611.21533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21533)). Jiazheng now returns to Southeast University in Nanjing, China, to continue his PhD research. We wish Jiazheng best of luck for the future!
