A warm welcome to Yang Zhao, who joins us this month to begin his PhD studies, supervised by Dr Yang Yang, Dr Wei Wei, and Prof John Cartlidge. Yang recently completed our [MSc Financial Technology with Data Science](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/taught/msc-financial-technology-with-data-science/). His PhD research focuses on the market structure of centralised and decentralised financial markets, with a particular interest in digital assets and tokenised real-world assets.
